FloSure

Continuous vascular monitoring for dialysis care.

Dialysis is a demanding routine, and vascular access complications can interrupt treatment. FloSure is designed to help detect clotting risk earlier with wearable monitoring between sessions.

4 hoursa day
3 daysa week
52 weeksa year
The Problem

Dialysis access can clot between treatments.

More than 550,000 dialysis patients are at risk of clotting, creating a need for better visibility outside the dialysis session.

550,000+

dialysis patients at risk of clotting

Patients spend hours each week in dialysis, but the access site is not continuously monitored between sessions. FloSure focuses on closing that monitoring gap.

The Solution

Meet FloGuard.

A compact wearable device designed to monitor a dialysis access site using complementary sensing technologies.

Wearable monitoring built around the access site

FloGuard combines an electronics board with Doppler ultrasound and a photoacoustic sensor inside a waterproof housing.
